Name: Pétanque Counter

Short desc: Keep pétanque scores on phone and/or watch

Full desc:
Since in this era you always have your phone or Wear OS smartwatch with you, why not use it to keep the score.
- simple interface
- multiple phone layouts (symmetric, one-hand-left, one-hand-right)
- stays above/outside the phone lock screen for simple access
- Wear OS: standalone scoring, 1–6 points per mène
